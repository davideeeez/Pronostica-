## Assistant

We've been developing a visual identity system for Pronostica!, a free Italian Serie A football prediction app targeting 18-45 year old fans. The goal is to create 4-6 distinct brand directions that are competitive and youthful but credible—similar in register to Fantamondiale (Fantacalcio × DAZN) but visually distinct.

We started with your reference screenshot (hero banner, stat cards, bottom navigation structure) and built four initial directions: Notturna (night blue + acid green), Tabellino (field green + rotational red), Diretta (electric blue + magenta), and Curva (hot orange + warm black). You then asked for two more directions closer to your reference, so we added Tribuna (navy + warm yellow on light) and Prima Fila (cobalt + lime yellow on night background), both with fresh, rounded typefaces.

Each direction includes a full palette (hex codes), typographic pairing, three descriptive adjectives, and applied mockups across hero, stat card, button, and bottom nav. All six are displayed side-by-side on the canvas for comparison.

Most recently, we converted the original design file into a deck-stage slide presentation (8 slides, 1920×1080) with one slide per direction showing palette, type samples, tone descriptors, and applied mockups. This was exported as a PPTX for presentation.

The original canvas (Pronostica Brand Directions.dc.html) remains intact with all six directions. Next steps would be choosing a preferred direction and deepening it—logo exploration, full screen flows, interaction states, or component library.

Files: Pronostica Brand Directions.dc.html, Pronostica Brand Deck.dc.html, Pronostica Brand Deck.pptx
